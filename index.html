II Manifesto · what we believe

The web doesn't need another agency. It needs three people who care about typography, ship on time, and pick up the phone.

We started Triadic because the cheap end of the market ships ugly templates, the expensive end ships beautiful PDFs with bloated invoices, and the middle is empty.

Our entire studio is built on one bet: that solo professionals will pay fair money for the kind of editorial design usually reserved for Series A startups — if someone will just deliver it without the theatre.

IV Anti-pattern · what we won't do

Things we won't sell you.

Half of what makes a studio worth hiring is the work it refuses. Here are the six we refuse.

✕
Discovery sprints.

Discovery is one 30-minute call. If we need more than that to understand a brochure site, we're padding the invoice.

✕
Hourly billing.

Ever. Hourly punishes you for our learning curve. Fixed price means the only person betting on our speed is us.

✕
Subcontracting.

The three names on this site are the three people building yours. No offshore handoffs, no white-label partners.

✕
Redesigns without rewriting.

A new template over old copy is a worse site, slowly. We rewrite, or we don't take it.

✕
More than two projects at once.

The math is simple. Two live, one in queue. If we say yes to your project, you have our attention — not a slot in a backlog.

✕
E-commerce.

Brochure sites are our craft. For Shopify or anything with a real cart, we'll introduce you to people who do that well.

V Process · five steps

How a site actually ships.

Five steps. Two to three weeks. Two revision rounds baked in. No mystery meetings, no scope drift, no late invoices.

01 / Discovery
A 30-minute call.
You talk, we listen. Brand, audience, the thing your last designer didn't understand. We take notes.
Day 0
02 / Quote
Fixed, in 48 hours.
One number. Scope attached. The price doesn't move unless you ask for something new — and even then, you see the delta first.
Day 2
03 / Design
One week. Two rounds.
You see the homepage first, in Figma. We revise twice. We almost never need a third — but if we do, it's on us.
Days 3–9
04 / Build
One week. You watch it grow.
A staging URL you can refresh. Daily progress. Comments answered in a working day, every time.
Days 10–16
05 / Ship
Live, with handoff docs.
We push to your domain. We hand you a one-page guide for updates. First month of small tweaks is free.

VIII FAQ · the objections

The hard questions.

These are the six things every prospect asks before they email. Here they are, answered honestly.

Q.01Why fixed price?

Because hourly billing punishes you for our learning curve. Fixed price means we eat the risk if we underestimate — and you get a number you can plan around.

Q.02Do you handle hosting?

Yes. We set up Vercel or Netlify hosting and walk you through transferring it to your own account. Or we manage it for $15/month if you'd rather not think about it.

Q.03What if I need changes after launch?

The first month of small tweaks is free. After that, it's $50/hour for one-offs, or a $400 monthly retainer if you want ongoing changes and someone on call.

Q.04Do you build e-commerce?

No. We focus on brochure-style sites — service businesses, portfolios, personal brands. For Shopify or anything with a real cart, we'll point you to people who do it well.

Q.05Why is your team in India?

Because we deliver Pentagram-tier work at a third of US agency prices. Our async-first process means time zones are a feature, not a bug — you sleep, we ship.

Q.06What if I hate the design?

Two free revision rounds are baked in. After that, additional rounds are $100 each. We almost never need them — but the option is there if you do.
