I The pitch · in three lines
01

Fixed price.

No hourly surprises. You see the number before you commit. We eat the risk if we underestimate — that's the deal.

02

Two to three weeks.

Not two to three months. We build small, focused sites that ship — and we ship on a calendar, not a vibe.

03

Three people.

Not one freelancer juggling eight clients. Not an agency burying you in account managers. Three specialists. One project at a time.

II Manifesto · what we believe

The web doesn't need another agency. It needs three people who care about typography, ship on time, and pick up the phone.

We started Triadict because the cheap end of the market ships ugly templates, the expensive end ships beautiful PDFs with bloated invoices, and the middle is empty.

Our entire studio is built on one bet: that solo professionals will pay fair money for the kind of editorial design usually reserved for Series A startups — if someone will just deliver it without the theatre.

V Process · five steps

How a site actually ships.

Five steps. Two to three weeks. Two revision rounds baked in. No mystery meetings, no scope drift, no late invoices.

01 / Discovery
A 30-minute call.
You talk, we listen. Brand, audience, the thing your last designer didn't understand. We take notes.
Day 0
02 / Quote
Fixed, in 48 hours.
One number. Scope attached. The price doesn't move unless you ask for something new — and even then, you see the delta first.
Day 2
03 / Design
One week. Two rounds.
You see the homepage first, in Figma. We revise twice. We almost never need a third — but if we do, it's on us.
Days 3–9
04 / Build
One week. You watch it grow.
A staging URL you can refresh. Daily progress. Comments answered in a working day, every time.
Days 10–16
05 / Ship
Live, with handoff docs.
We push to your domain. We hand you a one-page guide for updates. First month of small tweaks is free.
Day 17

VII Pricing · three ranges

The price is on the page.

No "request a quote" theatre. No three-tier comparison games. Our work falls in three ranges depending on scope. Every project gets a fixed quote within 48 hours of the discovery call — the price doesn't move once we agree.

Essentials

$800 – $1,500 USD

One-page sites and focused brochures. For solo professionals who need one beautiful page that does its job — fast, clean, accessibility-first.

  • Solo realtors
  • Therapists
  • Coaches
  • Personal brands

Standard

$1,500 – $3,000 USD

Multi-page editorial sites with depth. Case studies, blog systems, lead capture, schema markup — everything a small business needs to look like a serious one.

  • Boutique services
  • Small studios
  • Consultants
  • Real-estate teams

Bespoke

$3,000+ USD

Custom briefs that don't fit a range. Complex scope, specific integrations, multi-language sites, or ongoing partnerships. Quoted case-by-case.

  • Established brands
  • Multi-stakeholder briefs
  • Long-term retainers
Every quote is fixed · No hourly · No surprises · No retainer lock-ins
